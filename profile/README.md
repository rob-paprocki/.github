# Hi there 👋

## 💫 About Me
🔭 I’m currently working on a lot of things!<br><br>🤝 I’m looking for help with bug hunting.<br><br>Hi, I’m Rob Paprocki. I’m a 30-year-old audio engineer, musician, and relentless technology enthusiast with a deep foundation in professional AV. I hold an AVIXA CTS certification and a Bachelor of Music in Music Production from The Hartt School at The University of Hartford, but my curiosity has always pushed me to look beyond the audio console.<br><br>As a highly motivated self-starter, I have never outgrown the urge to tinker and figure out how things operate. My work and personal projects span a wide spectrum – from physically wiring and commissioning enterprise electronics to exploring self-hosted software packages and experimenting with generative AI.<br><br>People often use the phrase “jack of all trades, master of none,” but I firmly believe that continuous, hands-on learning allows for genuine mastery across multiple disciplines. Expanding my technical toolkit allows me to adapt quickly, solve complex problems, and ultimately get more done.
